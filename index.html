01 — About

A biochemist with a soft spot for code

First and foremost, I'm a biochemist and laboratory scientist. I've spent years purifying proteins, culturing cells, and chasing clean data — the lab is home, and the analytical, detail-obsessed mindset that comes with it is how I approach everything.

More recently I've fallen for the problem-solving rush of writing code — very much as an enthusiast still, learning as I go. And when I'm away from the bench or the keyboard, you'll find me near the water. Marine biology is my forever fascination, and yes, I will happily talk about dolphins for far longer than is socially acceptable.

"The good thing about science is that it's true whether or not you believe in it — and the ocean keeps most of it hidden."
